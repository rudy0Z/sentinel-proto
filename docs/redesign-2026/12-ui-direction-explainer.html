Why this layout

One source of truth, drawn twice

The left attention spines answer “did anything change?” Each lip carries its one live value, so a collapsed panel is never blind. The map stage is the artifact the operator watches, not a widget beside chrome. The TALON Mind thread shows the reasoning: tool trace, evidence chips, plan cards. The gate at the deck center is the only place a consequential action is authorized.

The hot edge is the law: anything actionable in the thread mirrors at the gate, from the same derivation. Chat and orb can never disagree. This fixes the split-brain without moving the gate, and it keeps the 2-second hold on a surface that never slides.

SciChart — patterns, not license

Steal the strip-chart window with its “now” line, threshold reference marks, and shared crosshair. The scripted data never needs WebGL charting; uPlot covers the strip case.

hairline — custom assets

Isometric line figures of the TALON mark and the dock for the case study and empty states. Line-drawn, pointer-reactive, MIT.

not adopted

shadcn-map (Leaflet loses the elastic camera), PaceUI (Pro-gated), and the inspiration registries. Browse, don't build.

Build order

Fix the broken controls before styling anything

the real risk

Three half-finished shells. The theme pass must land on the promoted shell, not beside it. Pick the v2 layout, promote it, and restyle once.

honesty law

Age badges on stale overlays. Expiry never executes. Charts show what the data is. The redesign must not decorate around these.

done when

The owner walks verify-active → rescue-battery-critical, finishes the 2-second hold without losing the spine, and every gate still reads green.
