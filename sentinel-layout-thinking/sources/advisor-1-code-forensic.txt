### If TALON becomes the information bus, what layouts are possible?

Constraint first: `00-INDEX:76-84` + golden rules are non-negotiable. TALON never controls app directly, staged expires to watch, gated actions need human hold. Any layout that makes "chat sends command" breaks the constitution. And desk-panel vs tablet changes everything:

* Desk-embedded panel (low angle, arm's reach, glare): bottom-center wins. Hands rest at desk edge, 56px pills are touchable, center is equidistant. Right edge is far.
* Handheld tablet (portrait, thumbs): bottom-center loses, right-dock loses. You need single-column + bottom sheet. Current 260-340px rails collapse badly here — that's why `DESIGN.md:171-176` pins 1920x1080 as acceptance, not tablet.

Options I evaluated:

**A. Your proposal: Right-docked TALON chat + actions, thin bottom strip**
Right rail becomes full-height `TALON conversation`: transcript, chips (`Why now? / Confidence? / What's blocked?`), evidence inline, action buttons at bottom of chat. Left rail becomes unified Intel (inspector + priorities + fleet). Bottom reduces to 44px status + hold pill only.

*Pro:* accessible, connected — question and answer and button live together. Familiar chat mental model, great for mouse/tablet-landscape. Fixes the split-brain.
*Con:* loses gate geometry. Chat normalizes "TALON said → I clicked" as just another message. The aperture-closed-stillness cue dies in a scrolling list. Also puts authorization at far right — worst reach on desk-panel. And chat history invites hallucination trust; current brief format (Assessment → Evidence → Gate, max 90 words per `04 §6`) was designed to prevent that.

**B. Keep center, make rails feed it (dynamic hub)**
Rails stay, but become collapsible sources. TALON bottom becomes expandable: quiet = pill only, working = voice line, gated = voice + pills + evidence drawer rising. Right rail `Workforce` moves *into* the expanded session as DAG, not a separate tab.

*Pro:* preserves symmetry + gate + map dominance. Smallest code change. Portfolio embed focal contract survives.
*Con:* doesn't solve "I want to talk to TALON." Still no persistent conversation.

**C. Classic C2: left inspect, center map, right TALON-ops full height**
What Palantir/Anduril-style consoles do. Right rail owns recommendation + packet + hold button stacked vertically. Bottom bar deleted entirely.

*Pro:* one vertical scan: see → understand → authorize, top to bottom. Best for fixed desk monitor.
*Con:* map shrinks ~340px permanently. Authorization scrolls below fold — violates `DESIGN.md:335` one dominant command rule. Touch target moves to mid-right, awkward on desk.

**D. My recommendation: split TALON in two — voice stays center, conversation moves right**

Keep `deriveTalonSurface()` orb + voice + bloomed pills at bottom-center exactly as-is. That's the authorization instrument, not chat.

But promote right `TALON` tab from brief to true dynamic interface:

1. Top: same `IntelligenceBrief` (it already works).
2. Middle: real conversation thread — transcript, `TalonStream` replies, scene chips from `domain/talon.ts:68-90`, not hidden in overlay.
3. Bottom (sticky): context action — mirrors whatever pill is bloomed at center. Click either place, same `handleAction`. So chat and orb never disagree.

Left rail simplifies to `Inspect / Assets / Ledger` only. `Priorities` moves out — Intelligence priorities already duplicate TALON recommendation, Operations priorities duplicate route state. Kill the duplication, keep rails from competing.

Defense vs existing:

* Existing defends map dominance and gate clarity, but concedes connectedness — you have to look bottom for what, right for why.
* Full right-move defends connectedness, but concedes gate authority, desk reach, and the 8-state mark language that took `09` to justify.
* Split defends both: center keeps `human authorization is structural` legible at 200ms glance (`09 §6`), right gains the chat+buttons accessibility you want, and backend Phase 5 maps cleanly — `TalonConverser` feeds right thread, `StateResolver` still gates center pills. No layout needs to know about LLM nondeterminism.
