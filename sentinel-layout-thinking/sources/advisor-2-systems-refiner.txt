Where it gets futuristic is the TALON rule, and this is where you must be strict:

Current law from `04-TALON-AI-AGENT-CONSTITUTION.md:59-64`: Rule of One + Anti-Nagging. If operator rejects, TALON stays silent until `reRaiseThreshold` crosses. Your "popup into active state" cannot mean auto-expand on every update, or you rebuild Clippy with a wildfire.

Attention protocol I'd enforce:
